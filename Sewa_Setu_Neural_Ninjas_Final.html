1. Key Findings & Strategic Context

Digital governance in India has evolved from basic digitization (PDF forms and physical kiosk submission) to deep, interoperable Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI). While Chhattisgarh Sewa Setu (implemented under CHiPS) has made significant strides by introducing WhatsApp-assisted tracking, digital QR certificates, and Bhashini multilingual translation, comparative research against top-performing states reveals key architectural bottlenecks:

  • Department-Siloed Navigation: Sewa Setu forces citizens to guess which government department owns a service (e.g., Revenue vs. General Administration). Best-practice platforms like Goa Online and Odisha One utilize intent-based selection wizards ("What life event are you navigating?").
  • Opaque SLA Tracking: While Sewa Setu publishes statutory SLAs (e.g., 7 days for Domicile/Income, 22 days for OBC Caste Certificates), citizens are only given static ARN status strings without granular step-by-step progress. Goa Online features a live public Turnaround Time (TAT) Dashboard, while Karnataka Seva Sindhu enforces legislative compensation under the SAKALA Act.
  • Repetitive Document Submissions: Citizens are required to re-upload documents (Misal records, Vanshavali, Revenue receipts) manually for every application. Deep integration with DigiLocker API (pulling verified records and pushing signed certificates) remains underutilized.
  • Equity & Fee Waivers: Sewage Setu applies uniform application fees regardless of socio-economic status. Kerala e-District automatically connects to state PDS databases to waive/reduce fees for BPL citizens at Akshaya Kendras.

2. Current State Analysis: Chhattisgarh Sewa Setu Today

What Exists TODAY (Verified)

  • Nodal Agency: CHiPS (Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society).
  • Services covered: Domicile, Income, Birth, Death, Caste Certificates (SC/ST/OBC), Revenue services.
  • Digital Certificates with embedded QR codes for instant verification.
  • WhatsApp Bot for basic tracking & certificate delivery.
  • Bhashini API integration for real-time translation.

What We PROPOSE (Our Solution)

  • Agentic AI Intent Search ("What do you need?") replacing department menus.
  • E-commerce style step-by-step visual timeline for application tracking.
  • Automated BPL fee relief logic engine tied to PDS database.
  • DigiLocker push/pull single-click document auto-fill.
  • Public TAT Analytics Dashboard & SLA Breach Heatmap for eDMs.
